I'm a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [University of Maryland](https://cs.umd.edu/). Previously, I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Physics from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/).

My research focuses on Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI agents, with three main directions:

- **Self-Evolving Agentic Systems:** Enabling agents to learn from experience, adapt to new tasks and environments, and continuously improve their capabilities.
- **Efficient and Trustworthy Automation:** Understanding how agents can autonomously complete complex tasks under various constraints while balancing independent decision-making with human oversight.
- **Multi-Agent Collaboration:** Understanding when and why multiple agents are needed, and how they can effectively coordinate and collaborate to solve complex tasks.

In addition, I'm also interested in how to design more efficient and effective evaluation methodologies for LLMs and AI agents, and the broader application of AI agents in real-world scenarios.
